How to read this honestly

  • “Passes” means the validator found no rule violations. It cannot judge whether image descriptions are meaningful, whether the reading order makes sense on a complicated page, or whether a table is sensible. A person still looks over each file.
  • “After” is before any human work. Once the image descriptions are written, the files that only lacked those pass too (shown live in the demo on a short document).
  • Two of the ten were already fine (one Word report, one InDesign guide). The tool leaves good files alone.
  • Ten publications is a small sample. A pilot on 30 to 50 documents is the next honest step.
  • Everything ran offline on one computer; nothing was uploaded anywhere.
